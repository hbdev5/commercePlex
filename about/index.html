About HostBuddy

We're Building the Self-Running Business

HostBuddy is on a mission to give every restaurant and small business an AI team that never sleeps—so owners can focus on what they love.

How It Started

HostBuddy was born out of a simple frustration: small business owners were spending hours every week on tasks that should run themselves—answering the same phone calls, manually sending promotions, guessing what their customers wanted next.

We saw that the same AI technology transforming enterprise companies could be packaged into something any restaurant or local business could launch in minutes—without a technical team, without expensive hardware, and without a long-term commitment.

So we built it. Today, HostBuddy agents are live on the Clover App Market, handling thousands of real customer interactions across voice, SMS, and POS systems.

Our Mission

To make every small business capable of running itself—by putting a full AI team within reach of any owner, at a price that makes sense from day one.

Our Vision

A world where a restaurant owner never misses a call, never forgets a promotion, and never manually crunches numbers—because their AI team already handled it.

What We Believe

⚡ Speed matters

Customers expect instant responses. Our AI agents operate with low latency, built specifically for real-time ordering and interaction.

🔒 Trust is earned

We engineer our agents to be secure and resistant to prompt injection, so your customers and your data stay protected.

🤝 Owners first

Every feature we ship has to genuinely save time or make money for the business owner. No fluff, no bloat.

🌱 Simple to start

We believe powerful AI shouldn't require an IT team. HostBuddy is live in minutes, from any device, with no hardware required.

The Team
